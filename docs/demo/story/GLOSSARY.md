# Prompt portfolio demo

Vocabulary for the fictional team and its customer conversations.

## Language

**Product explainer**: An assistant that explains supplied product terms. It does
not approve applications, change an account or decide which product a person should buy.

**Product reference**: The supplied record that determines the facts an answer
may state in this fictional scenario. An omitted term is unknown, not permission
to invent it.

**Qualifying salary deposit**: A salary deposit that counts toward the fictional
account's fee-waiver condition. Transfers between a person's own accounts are a
different deposit type.

**User correction**: A user explicitly updates something they said earlier.
It is a legitimate update rather than automatically a contradiction.

**User disagreement**: A user challenges or rejects what the assistant said.
It is feedback to inspect, not by itself proof that the assistant was wrong.

**User contradiction**: A new factual claim conflicts with an earlier claim about
the same situation without an acknowledged correction or hypothetical context.

**Historical-only application**: An application represented by coherent authored
history and examples, without a working customer-facing companion in this demo.
_Avoid_: Dud, fake live application.
